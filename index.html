Pond monitoring · Pollution reporting

Report pond pollution. Track it until it is fixed.

JALACHAYA connects citizens, the Panchayat office and field workers, so every complaint about a village pond is recorded, assigned, cleaned up and then checked by the community before it is closed.

01 · The problem

Why JALACHAYA

Village ponds are used for bathing, irrigation and fishing, but complaints about waste, sewage or weed growth are often made verbally and then forgotten. There is no record of who was responsible, whether the work was done, or whether the problem came back.

One record per complaint

Every report gets a complaint ID, a photo and a full history that cannot be quietly deleted.

Clear responsibility

Each task has a named field worker and a deadline. Overdue work is flagged and escalated.

Community verification

Work is only marked verified after residents confirm the problem is actually solved.

02 · The process

How it works

Six steps from a citizen's report to a pond that the community agrees is clean again.

  1. ReportA citizen selects the pond, describes the problem and attaches a photo.
  2. Review and assignThe Panchayat office reviews it and assigns a field worker with a deadline.
  3. Field workThe worker inspects the pond, records notes and carries out the work.
  4. EvidenceBefore and after photos are uploaded and checked by the Panchayat.
  5. Community checkResidents confirm the work, or dispute it and the complaint is reopened.
  6. Workflow trackingTrack each pond’s complaints from reporting to community verification.
